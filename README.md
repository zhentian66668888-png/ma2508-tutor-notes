# MA2508 Tutor Notes

Selected teaching materials from my student tutor work in multivariable calculus. These samples show how I organize a tutorial, pair Chinese and English explanations, and build mathematical reasoning step by step.

## Teaching approach

The teaching plan allocates time across problems, highlights common mistakes and includes questions to check understanding. The bilingual script pairs mathematical explanations in Chinese and English. Handwritten notes illustrate the derivations used during problem explanations.

## Materials

| Material | What to look for |
|---|---|

| [Bilingual teaching script](materials/bilingual-teaching-script.pdf) | Explanations, transitions and guided questions in Chinese and English |
| [Handwritten derivations](materials/handwritten-notes.pdf) | Chain rule, directional derivatives, tangent planes, limits and constrained extrema |

## Learning goals

- Choose the right differentiation or limit method and explain why it applies.
- Connect geometric interpretations to formulas and calculations.
- Check assumptions and identify common errors in multivariable calculus.

This is a selected teaching portfolio. Third-party exercise booklets, full examination collections and separate complete solution sets are excluded. Some explanations refer to course problems; these references do not claim original authorship of the underlying questions.
