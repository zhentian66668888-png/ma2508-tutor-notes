# MA2508 Tutor Notes

Tutorial questions, worked solutions and teaching materials from my student tutor work in multivariable calculus. These samples show how I organize a tutorial, pair Chinese and English explanations, and build mathematical reasoning step by step.

## Teaching approach

The teaching plan allocates time across problems, highlights common mistakes and includes questions to check understanding. The bilingual script pairs mathematical explanations in Chinese and English. Handwritten notes illustrate the derivations used during problem explanations.

## Learning goals

- Choose the right differentiation or limit method and explain why it applies.
- Connect geometric interpretations to formulas and calculations.
- Check assumptions and identify common errors in multivariable calculus.

The complete midterm tutorial question sheet and its worked solutions are included alongside the teaching notes, script and handwritten derivations. Third-party exercise booklets and original examination collections are kept separately. Some explanations refer to course problems; these references do not claim original authorship of the underlying questions.
